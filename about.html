ABOUT US

BOYNEXTDOOR carries the meaning of "the boys next door" and is KOZ's first boy group, consisting of six members: Sungho, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Leehan, and Woonhak. Just like their friendly and familiar name, they aim to share everyday stories and express the wide range of emotions within them through easy-to-listen yet unique music that seeks to create connection.


BOYNEXTDOOR believes that the essence of music lies in the exchange of emotions. When you play a song, you naturally find yourself listening closely, walking along to lyrics that feel like your own story, awakening forgotten emotions, and reflecting on moments in your life. This sort of empathy is what BOYNEXTDOOR strives to reach with their music.


Because of this, the free-spirited and honest BOYNEXTDOOR focuses on telling the stories of youth in this current.

BOYNEXTDOOR Official Fandom Name: ONEDOOR (원도어)

Fandom Name Meaning: The fans of BOYNEXTDOOR are the only ONEDOOR that can connect BOYNEXTDOOR to the world. With ONEDOOR, BOYNEXTDOOR will open the door to the bigger world and move forward towards a shared dream and visions for the future.
